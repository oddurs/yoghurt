## Problem

"What does my laptop have that the desktop does not" is a real question, and the
inventory needed to answer it already exists.

## Proposal

Export the graph and diff two exports. Parked as a genuinely new product surface
rather than a completion of this one — exactly the kind of exciting thing that
does not belong in v1.0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A stable export format, versioned
- [ ] A diff that reports present-only, missing, and version-differing
